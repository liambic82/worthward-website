BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

Can I use Worthward for free?

Yes. Core shift tracking and basic forecasting are free. Optional Pro features are available through a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No account is required to get started, and there are no ads. Read the privacy policy for details about how data is handled.

Is this my take-home pay?

No. The estimates shown here are gross earnings, before taxes and other deductions. They depend on the hours and pay rules you enter and may differ from your employer’s payroll.

Can I track more than one workplace?

Yes. Keep separate workplace profiles and pay rules, so you can track shifts for different jobs.
